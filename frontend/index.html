GOOD TO KNOW

Questions,
answered.

Do I need an account? +

No. MediaDrop is ready to use without an account, login, email, or subscription.

Can I download any public video? +

Not necessarily. Public visibility does not guarantee automated downloading. We only process supported URLs through permitted methods.

Can I preview a video first? +

Yes, when a browser-playable preview source is legitimately available. Otherwise, you will see a clear preview-unavailable message.

Can I download a transcript? +

Yes, for supported videos with available subtitles or captions. MediaDrop will surface a transcript download when the source provides one and the content is accessible.

Does MediaDrop bypass access controls? +

No. Private content, DRM, paywalls, CAPTCHA, logins, and other access controls are never bypassed.
